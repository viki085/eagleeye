Expenses FAQ

Submitting Claims

Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the purchase. Receipts are required for any item over EUR 25. Upload receipts in the Concur mobile app.

Records related to this topic are kept in the system of record named in the appendix. Copies held elsewhere, such as in personal folders or chat messages, are not considered authoritative and may be out of date.

The owning team publishes a short changelog whenever this guidance changes, and important changes are announced in the monthly all-hands meeting and in the company newsletter.

Where this guidance refers to amounts, they include tax unless stated otherwise. Amounts in other currencies are converted using the rate published by Finance at the start of each quarter.

This section is reviewed every year by the policy owner together with Legal and People Operations. Where local law sets a stricter rule, the local rule applies and the regional lead records the exception in the policy register.

Managers are expected to explain these rules to new joiners during their first week and to point them to the internal portal, where the latest version of every policy is published alongside a short summary and a change history.

Questions that are not answered here should be raised with the relevant service desk through the usual ticketing channel. Please include enough context for the team to act without a follow-up conversation.

Specific Items

Client gifts are limited to EUR 50 per person. Home internet is reimbursed at EUR 30 per month for remote employees. Taxis are allowed after 21:00 or when carrying equipment. Conference fees need pre-approval from your budget owner. Fines such as parking tickets are never reimbursed.
