# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity Levels

SEV1 means a full outage affecting all customers. SEV2 covers degraded service for a subset of customers. SEV3 is a minor issue with a workaround.

Teams in different countries sometimes apply these rules differently in practice. The intent is always the same: be reasonable, be transparent, and ask early when in doubt rather than asking for forgiveness later.
